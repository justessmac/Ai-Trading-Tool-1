## 2026-10-02: lessons from options-mode day 2 (NKE exit and empty afternoon)
1. **Peak was the opening print.** NKE puts: bid $1.21 at 09:32 (+135%), sold $0.69 at 09:35 (+23%). ~$208 left on the table (4 x $0.52). Cause: the 9:31 routine prompt said "do NOT trade in the first minutes", which conflicted with "sell at the peak". Fix (user-approved): SELL AT THE OPEN for earnings plays up >= +100%; routine step (0) now runs it first. General lesson: when two rules can conflict, write down which one wins *before* the event.
2. **The scanner was looking at the wrong universe.** Rel-volume/price scans returned leveraged ETFs, bond funds, microcaps with no options, and $400-$800 stocks whose affordable strikes are illiquid (STX, WDC). Hours went into names that could never pass the option filters. Fix: scan for *option* liquidity directly: `optionsCallDayVolume + optionsPutDayVolume >= 5000`, price $3-80, rel volume >= 1.5. Tested at 15:40: it surfaces tradeable movers (VSH +10%, STM +7%, VIAV +6.5%, PENG +13%, WOLF, IBRX) and drops the junk.
3. **Size the underlying to the account.** With ~$300, a usable contract costs <= ~$300, so underlyings above ~$100 only offer far-OTM lottery strikes with wide spreads. Prefer $5-80 underlyings.
4. **Recognize pinned stocks fast.** SYNA +14% was a cash takeover at $123: capped upside, calls are a bad bet. Rule: skip names whose move comes from a fixed-price cash deal.
5. **Event timing of entries.** Buying Friday afternoon pays two days of weekend decay; earnings plays are best entered the day before the report.
6. **Routine hygiene.** Peak-watch one-offs kept firing after the position was sold; future one-offs should be cancelled right after the exit.
7. **Watch the deposit.** The $250 pending deposit disappeared from pending_deposits without landing; flagged to the user.
