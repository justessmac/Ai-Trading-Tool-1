# Turn-of-month effect and T-bill yield for idle cash

**Verdict (2026-10-01): adopted as Strategy 4, using idle cash only (max 20% of the account). Idle cash otherwise goes in SGOV.**
- The in-sample pick (hold SPY over the last 4 and first 1 trading days of each month, no trend filter) made +0.53%/trade (t 2.7, 168 trades) in 2001-2014. It held up on 2015-2026: +0.39%/trade (t 2.5, 140 trades, 59% win).
- Neighbouring settings also pass OOS (4/2, 4/3, 4/4: t 2.6-2.9), and the same rule works on QQQ and IWM. So it is not a lucky parameter.
- Honest size: a random 5-day SPY hold made +0.25% in 2015-2026, so the extra edge is about +0.14%/trade. Most of the gain is ordinary market exposure, taken only on ~24% of days.
- As an idle-cash sleeve in 2015-2026: BIL + TOM made +5.3%/yr (max DD -10%), vs +2.0% for T-bills only and 0% for cash.
- SMA200-filtered versions looked good in-sample but faded OOS (t 0.3-1.9), so no filter.

SPY daily (dividend-adjusted) 2000-01-03 to 2026-09-25. Costs 0.03%/side. Selection on 2001-2014, confirmation on 2015-2026.
